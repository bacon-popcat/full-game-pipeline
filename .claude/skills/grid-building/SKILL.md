# Grid building

The world is cut into 3-stud cells. A placeable fills whole cells, sits on its origin cell's
floor and turns in quarter turns about Y. Read [docs/inventory.md](../../../docs/inventory.md)
(Block templates) and the headers of [src/shared/Build.luau](../../../src/shared/Build.luau),
`BuildService` and `BuildController`.

## The template contract

This contract is the core of the system. Most placement bugs were a template breaking it.

- A template lives in `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.Blocks`, named by item id.
- **Its `PrimaryPart` is its hitbox**: a box of whole cells. Its size *is* the footprint and
  its centre *is* the one pivot. A cube is its own hitbox; a machine has an invisible,
  queryable, non-colliding `Hitbox` part around its art.
- Art may poke past the hitbox by at most 0.15 studs. `Build`'s `TOUCH_MARGIN` (0.2)
  allows for that.
- Footprints and pivots come **from the hitbox, never from the bounding box**. Rounding up a
  bounding box shifted off-centre art half a cell and broke conveyor and tube lines.
- A template whose hitbox isn't whole cells warns and can't be placed. That is deliberate.
- Hitboxes are generated by the art pipeline (`IslandsMachines/build/studio_blocks.luau`;
  see `blender-studio-assets`), which also writes `AuthoredOrigin` and `PortDepth*`. Rerun it
  after any import.

## Placement rules

- **Test the world, keep no occupancy map.** A placement is legal when it is within `REACH`,
  nothing queryable but characters and loose drops overlaps its shrunken box, and the block's
  own colliding parts don't clip a character (`Build.clipsCharacter` tests a shrunken copy of
  the art). With no map, nothing can fall out of sync as blocks are placed, broken or restored.
- Characters are tested against the **art**, not the box, so a player standing in the empty
  corner of a machine's cells can still place it. The user wanted characters to keep blocking
  placement: don't remove that.
- Loose physics items collide, so `CanQuery` can't hide them. Skip their folder by hand
  (`Build.isLoose`, `Aim.cast`).
- **The client proposes and the server re-checks**: the client sends a cell, a rotation and
  `against` (the axis of the surface it aimed at). The item always comes from the held slot.
  The server stores `ItemId`, `Owner`, `Origin`, `Rotation` and `Against` as attributes on the
  placed model in `Workspace.Placed`.
- **The ghost uses the same `Build` rules**: green means the server will accept it, red means
  it won't. Bind everything for the held item in one Trove (`heldTrove`) and clean it the
  moment the hand changes, so no ghost or binding is left behind.
- **Aim at hitboxes for placing, at art for describing.** Placing snaps to hitbox faces;
  hover text and the interact key use `Aim.castArt`, which passes through invisible hitboxes.
- **Ownership** (`garden-overhaul`): `Build.owns` checks the whole footprint and height
  against the player's plot; `Build.canEdit` also needs `DataReady`. Builds stay inside
  `y 0..79`.
- **Picking up**: only the owner can pick a block up. The Hammer takes any block; a cube also
  breaks with the tool named by its item's `breaksWith`. Its contents come back with it.

## Pieces that shape themselves

The Wood Tube shows the pattern for anything that joins its neighbours, such as pipes, rails,
fences or wires:

1. Make **one item**. The shapes (straight, corner, vertical, elbow up, elbow down) are art
   templates, not items.
2. On placing, link first to the block on the `Against` side, if it can take a link, then to
   any neighbour whose spare end points at the new piece, up to the link limit (2).
3. The links decide the ends, and the ends pick the art. With one link, the other end is
   straight across from it; with none, both ends run along the way it was placed facing.
4. Swap the art in under the placed model's Hitbox, turned to fit. When a piece is removed,
   reshape the neighbours it was linked to.
5. All shapes share one cross-section and sit on cell centre lines at the same height, so
   any two line up. That was fixed after a stack of verticals showed steps between pieces.

## Adding a placeable

1. Build the art inside whole cells, with every connecting end on a cell face (see
   `blender-studio-assets`).
2. Import it, run `studio_blocks.luau`, and check the template in `Assets.Blocks` has its Hitbox.
3. Add the item (`inventory-hotbar`) and, if it does something, a `Blocks` entry
   (`factory-automation`).
4. Test: place it in all four rotations, against each face, next to another of itself, while
   standing in its cells, at the edge of reach and, on `garden-overhaul`, at the plot edge.
